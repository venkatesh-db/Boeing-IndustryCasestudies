# Disputed payments and complaints

- A card payment can be disputed within 120 days of the payment date.
- The bank acknowledges a dispute within 2 working days and gives a final answer within 30 days.
- A duplicate charge (charged twice) is refunded within 5 working days after the dispute is confirmed.
- Complaints are handled by a trained complaints officer, not by the assistant. The customer can escalate to the external ombudsman if not satisfied.
