# Lost, stolen and blocked cards

- A lost or stolen card can be frozen at once in the mobile app under Cards > Freeze card.
- Freezing is reversible; cancelling a card is permanent and a new card is sent within 5 working days.
- The customer is not liable for card payments made after the card was reported lost or stolen.
- A card is blocked automatically after 3 wrong PIN attempts; it can be unblocked at any of the bank's ATMs.
