# Card and account fees

- Monthly account fee: 0 for balances above 1,000; otherwise 4.00 per month.
- Foreign card payments: 2.5% of the payment amount.
- ATM withdrawals abroad: 3.00 per withdrawal plus 1.5% of the amount.
- Replacement card: free once per year, then 10.00 per card.
- Late credit card payment: 25.00, charged once per statement.
